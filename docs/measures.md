# Measure definitions

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Renewable share (%) | Renewable generation divided by total generation, by country and year |
| Gap to target (points) | 2030 target share minus the latest renewable share |
| Required pace (points/year) | Gap to target divided by the years remaining to 2030 |
| Pace, last 5 years | Change in renewable share between 2020 and 2025 divided by five |
| Pace vs 2010–2015 | Pace over the last five years minus the average annual gain in 2010–2015 |
| Price difference (EUR/MWh) | Average Irish wholesale price minus the average price of the ten peer countries |

Worked example (2025): 80 − 48.37 = 31.63 points; 31.63 ÷ 5 = 6.33 points a year.

> DAX expressions live inside the `.pbix` file (Power BI Desktop → Model view / Data view). Export them with Tabular Editor or DAX Studio if you want them versioned as text.
